# Hijabi Bakes 🍰

A premium, modern, mobile-first landing page and interactive ordering website built specifically for **Hijabi Bakes**—a homebaker business based in Rajahmundry. Designed to drive direct sales without any backend, database, or complex frameworks, this site routes customer orders instantly to **WhatsApp** or **Instagram DM**.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* **Warm & Elegant Bakery Aesthetics:** Crafted with soft bakery tones (blush pink, caramel, cream, warm chocolate), high-end typography, and high-quality imagery.
* **Live Product Search & Filtering:** Customers can instantly search through treats or filter by categories (Cakes, Cupcakes, Pastries, Brownies, Cookies).
* **Interactive Multi-Item Order Form:** * Add multiple items dynamically with an "Add Another Item" button.
  * Real-time calculation of item subtotals, total quantity, and estimated price.
  * Pre-fills a clean, structured text summary for instant checkout.
* **Instant WhatsApp & Instagram Routing:** * Generates a direct `wa.me` chat link with pre-filled order details for WhatsApp.
  * Copies order details to the clipboard and redirects to Instagram DM.
* **Owner-Friendly Configuration:** The business owner can easily update shop info, phone numbers, social links, menu items, and pricing from a single configuration file (`script.js`) without touching HTML/CSS.
* **100% Static & Responsive:** Built with pure HTML5, CSS3, and modern JavaScript (no frameworks, libraries, or databases required).
